Advanced 3D Flight Simulator

Flight Controls

Mouse - Aim the plane

W/S or ↑/↓ - Pitch assist

A/D or ←/→ - Roll assist

Q/E - Yaw (rudder)

Shift - Increase throttle

Control - Decrease throttle

V - Toggle view (Cockpit/External)

R - Reset / recover after crash

Watch AGL (height above terrain). Buildings, towers, trees, and mountains are solid.
